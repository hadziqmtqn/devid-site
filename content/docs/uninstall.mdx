---
title: Uninstall
description: "Undo setup, with or without your data."
---

```sh
devid uninstall --dry-run      # show what would change
devid uninstall                # undo setup; keeps ~/.devid (PHP, config, CA, certificates)
devid uninstall --purge        # also delete ~/.devid
```

Uninstall undoes the system setup (with sudo), removes the LaunchAgents, the `PATH` lines (in `~/.zshenv` and `~/.zshrc`, or fish's `devid.fish`) and Devid.app, and deletes `~/.devid/bin`.

On Linux it also undoes the system setup with sudo. Where setup turned systemd-resolved on (Arch with NetworkManager), it puts `/etc/resolv.conf` back and turns systemd-resolved off again. It then removes Devid's certificate authority from Chrome's database and from each Firefox profile, the systemd user unit, and the `PATH` lines (in `~/.bashrc`, `~/.bash_profile` and `~/.profile`, and fish's `~/.config/fish/conf.d/devid.fish`), and deletes `~/.devid/bin`.
